Rules currently implemented
  • Attack choices: High Chop, Thrust, Low Slash.
  • Parry choices: High Parry, Parry, Low Parry.
  • A matching parry blocks the corresponding attack.
  • A failed parry makes the defender take a step back.
  • A successful parry reverses the roles.
  • Same-character starts are decided by coin flip.
  • Laputan Machine is fastest; Deadman is slowest.
  • Deadman gets an extra turn after a successful parry.
  • Against Deadman, a successful parry of Laputan stuns Laputan for one turn.

The supplied rules do not define a final victory condition, so this prototype temporarily ends a duel after 3 step-backs. Change this in server.js when you decide the real condition.
